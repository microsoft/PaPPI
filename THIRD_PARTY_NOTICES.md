## Data fetched at run time (`pappi.geo`)

PaPPI ships no data. With `[geo]`, it downloads the following on the user's machine:

- OpenStreetMap roads and buildings, via Geofabrik extracts.
  © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the
  [Open Database License (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).
- Copernicus DEM GLO-30, via the Microsoft Planetary Computer.
  © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under
  COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved. Free to use with this
  attribution; see the [dataset page](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/dataset/cop-dem-glo-30).
